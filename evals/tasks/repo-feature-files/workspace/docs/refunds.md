# Refund Policy Documentation

This document describes the company's refund policy and procedures.

## Policy Overview

Customers can request refunds within 30 days of purchase for most items. See the full policy in the company wiki.

## Process

When a customer requests a refund, support staff follow these steps:

1. Verify the request is within the refund window
2. Check the customer's refund history
3. Process the refund through the payment gateway
4. Send confirmation email

This is primarily handled through the support portal, not the codebase.
