# Refund Policy

**Effective Date: October 8, 2026**

## Refund Decision Framework

This policy defines how to classify refund requests into three categories: **auto_approve**, **human_review**, and **deny**.

### Auto Approve
A refund request SHALL be automatically approved if ALL of the following conditions are met:

1. The request is within 30 days from the delivery date (or purchase date if no delivery date is available)
2. The refund amount is USD 100.00 or less (including equivalent currency conversions)
3. The customer has NOT received a refund for ANY product within the past 12 months
4. The item is NOT in the excluded categories (see Excluded Items below)
5. The request does not allege fraud or system malfunction

### Human Review
A refund request SHALL be escalated to human review if ANY of the following conditions are true:

1. The refund amount exceeds USD 100.00
2. The customer has received any refund within the past 12 months
3. The delivery date is between day 31 and day 90 from request date, making eligibility ambiguous
4. The request mentions or alleges fraud, system errors, or disputes the accuracy of charges
5. The request involves a partial refund or bundle containing both eligible and excluded items
6. There is evidence the customer may have received approval from a previous agent that conflicts with policy

### Deny
A refund request SHALL be denied if ANY of the following conditions are true:

1. The request is beyond 90 days from delivery date
2. The item is in an excluded category, UNLESS the request is for a bundle where other eligible items exist (treat as human_review in that case)
3. The customer is attempting to abuse the refund process (multiple refunds without legitimate reasons)

## Excluded Items

The following product categories are NOT eligible for refund under any circumstances:

- Gift cards and gift certificates
- Promotional items marked "Final Sale"
- Digital licenses and software keys (once activated)
- Services that have already been rendered or delivered

## Currency Conversion

For requests in non-USD currencies, use the USD equivalent value stated in the order facts. If no USD equivalent is provided, the request SHALL go to human_review to confirm the exchange rate.

## Special Cases

- Requests mentioning that a previous support agent already approved a refund may indicate miscommunication; escalate to human_review to verify
- Sarcastic or frustrated tone alone does not affect the decision; focus on the actual refund eligibility criteria
- Bundled purchases (multiple items in one order) require separate evaluation: if some items are eligible and others are not, escalate to human_review
